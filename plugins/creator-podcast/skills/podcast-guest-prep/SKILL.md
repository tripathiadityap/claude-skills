# Podcast guest prep

Load `creator-profile` first for the show name, its promise and where it publishes. Use this before recording. After recording, hand off to `podcast-episode-package`.

## Inputs (ask only if missing)

Guest name, what they do, links (socials, site, past interviews), recording date, and which format: a long-form story conversation or a practical episode (pricing, contracts, money, rights, distribution).

## Workflow

1. **Research brief (internal, host only).** Search the web for the guest's work, public interviews and turning points. Summarise in your own words: who they are, 3 to 5 turning points or tensions in their story, topics they have been asked about to death, anything sensitive to avoid. Cite sources. Never invent biography details; mark unclear ones `[verify with guest]`.
2. **Episode angle.** A one-line spine: the single tension or turning point this conversation is about. Life and mindset first; career detail as context. It should fit the show's promise in the profile (for example: honest over polished, specific over motivational, human before heroic).
3. **Question run sheet** (60 to 90 minutes):
   - Warm open (2 to 3 questions): human, not CV.
   - The before: where they were, what was at stake.
   - The turn: the decision, the doubt, what it cost.
   - The messy middle: what nobody sees, what failed.
   - The now: what they believe now, what they'd tell someone with no map.
   - Rapid closing round (3 to 5 short questions).
   - 3 backup questions if a thread dies.
   Questions are open, specific to this guest, and skip the overused ones found in research.
   For a practical episode, swap the arc for: the problem, the numbers, the mistakes, the playbook.
4. **Guest-facing page.** Under 250 words: what the show is, the episode theme in one line, 4 to 6 topic areas (not the question list), format and length, logistics placeholders `[date / time / link]`, and where it will publish. No internal notes.

## Output

Research Brief, Episode Angle, Run Sheet, Guest Page. If the profile lists a Notion episode library, put the internal prep under that episode's entry and the guest page as a separate short page.

## Rules

- Guest-facing material stays short. Internal notes never double as guest-facing material.
- Do not reproduce long quotes from past interviews; paraphrase and link.
