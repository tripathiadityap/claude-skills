# YouTube packaging

Load `creator-profile` first. For turning a full episode into show notes, clip picks and posts across platforms, use `podcast-episode-package`; this skill goes deeper on YouTube packaging and the opening. Thumbnail and overlay text here is a brief for the designer.

Craft adapted from sergebulaev/youtube-skills, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Sergey Bulaev. Corpus findings are directional.

## What YouTube rewards (2026)

- Two halves: **P(click)** from title plus thumbnail, and **P(satisfied watch)** from retention, session time and returning viewers. A fast click-away after an over-promise is a heavy penalty.
- Retention is the spine. The first 30 seconds (3 on a Short) decide the curve.
- Shorts win reach, long-form wins depth and loyal subscribers. Use Shorts as the top of the funnel pointing at full episodes.
- Helps: chapters, end screens to a next episode, consistent packaging style, replying to early comments in the first hour, Test & Compare on titles and thumbnails.

## Before drafting

Need: what the episode or video actually delivers, the guest (and what they agreed can be said), the single most visual or dramatic moment, long-form or Short, and the main search phrase if any. Never invent a guest quote, a number, or a moment that is not in the video.

## Titles (always 3-5 variants)

| Code | Shape | Fit for the brand |
|---|---|---|
| Y1 | Curiosity gap with a concrete noun | a surprising guest claim (drop "what nobody tells you" phrasing) |
| Y3 | How-I outcome | the creator's transformation or a build they ran |
| Y5 | Transformation | before/after arcs over a timeframe |
| Y11 | Third-party success story | "How {guest or archetype} built {result} from {start}" (most frequent winning long-form shape in a 2026 pull) |
| Y2 / Y4 / Y6 | List, mistake, versus | weaker in creator/business niches; use for search-led explainers |

Rules: 40-60 characters ideal, 100 hard cap. Click-deciding words before any colon. One specific number where honest. No ALL CAPS, 0-1 emoji, no em dash (a colon reads cleaner). Never promise more than the first 30 seconds deliver. Title and thumbnail never repeat the same words. Flag which variant to lead the Test & Compare with.

## Thumbnail brief (2-3 concepts)

Each concept: angle and how it complements the title; focal subject (the creator or the guest with a real, legible emotion, or the object/result); overlay text of 4 words or fewer, no punctuation, never the title's words; background and contrast; composition (thirds, gaze direction); what must still read at 120 px on mobile. Vary one big element between concepts so the A/B test is readable. The emotion must match the video; a shocked face on a calm conversation is a broken promise.

## Opening hooks (the words said on camera)

- **Y7 Restate-and-Raise:** line 1 lands the title's promise in the creator's own words, line 2 raises the stakes, line 3 makes the watch contract ("by the end you'll know...").
- **Y8 Cold-Open Payoff Tease:** open on the guest's strongest real moment, then cut to setup. Default for podcast episodes. The clip must be in the episode.
- **Y9 Question-and-Contract:** ask the exact question the title implied, answer fast, reopen a deeper loop.
- **Y10 Shorts 3-Second Hook:** payoff or tension on frame one with on-screen text so it works muted; write the last line to loop back to the first.

No greeting, no logo sting, no "like and subscribe" in the first 30 seconds. Use `..` for spoken pauses on the teleprompter. Output beats with rough timestamps.

## Description

- **First ~150 characters:** a second hook with the payoff and the main search phrase. Never "welcome back".
- **Body (2-4 short paragraphs):** restate the promise, credit the guest properly, place keywords in natural sentences, the single most important link.
- **Furniture:** chapters starting at 0:00 (at least 3; long-form only), labelled links (Spotify, the newsletter, guest's handles), one specific subscribe reason, 3-5 hashtags at the very end.
- Under 5,000 characters.

## Community posts

Always copy-paste (no publishing API). Types: text tease, poll (ideas for future guests, settle a debate from the last episode), image + caption (a frame from the next episode), question prompt. Match the goal to the format.

## Channel audit

Check: channel name and handle consistent with the brand, banner that says what the channel is and the upload rhythm, description with the first lines as a pitch and a search phrase, channel trailer that opens with a hook (not a logo), playlists by show (full episodes, practical-format episodes, Shorts), consistent thumbnail style, end screens on every episode pointing to a next episode.

## Scrub before showing anything

No em dashes in titles, descriptions or overlays. None of: game-changer, unlock, leverage, "you won't believe", "this changes everything", "not gonna lie", "let me be honest", "here's what nobody tells you". No clickbait the video does not honour. Nothing from the profile's hard exclusions, no prices and no product-ladder pitches.

## Output

State the surface (long-form or Short) and what is being packaged, then the requested pieces with character counts, the recommended A/B lead, and any [placeholders].
