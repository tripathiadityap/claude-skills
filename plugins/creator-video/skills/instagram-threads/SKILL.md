# Instagram and Threads

Load `creator-profile` first. Use `content-calendar` to put finished pieces into the Notion calendar. The designer handles design; the creator owns angle, copy and approval, so slide and thumbnail text here is a brief for the designer, not final art.

Craft adapted from sergebulaev/instagram-skills and sergebulaev/threads-skills, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Sergey Bulaev. Signal notes are directional.

## Account routing

- **The personal account:** the creator's life, body, belief, lived experience. First person. Daily reel cadence.
- **The brand account:** shows, guests, launches, collaborations. Network voice, third person.
- **Any other account:** ask the creator what the piece is for before writing for it.
- **Threads:** runs off the Instagram identity. Confirm which handle before drafting.

## Personal-account series

When a reel idea comes in, decide which series it belongs to (or neither) before drafting. Keep the list of the creator's running series in `creator-profile`.

- **Default lane:** life experiences, quotes and mindset. Use the reel and caption formats below.
- **Numbered series (example: a daily rant series, "Day {N} of ..."):**
  - Title format on screen and in the caption: "Day {N} of {series}". Ask for the day number if it isn't given; never guess it.
  - One very specific, petty problem per episode, not a broad list. Specific beats general.
  - Self-aware humour: the joke is on the creator and their situation, never on people with less. No punching down.
  - Structure: "Day N" frame-one text, the exact problem stated flat in the first spoken line, the rant escalating with specific detail, a self-aware closer. Raw, no forced takeaway.
  - Goal: sends and comments ("send this to the friend who has this exact problem").
  - Caption: the problem in one line, the same tone, then a specific ask or an invite to suggest the next day's topic. Keep a running series tag in the hashtags.

## Instagram: what gets reach (2026)

- **Sends (DM shares) are the strongest signal**, then saves, comments with the creator replying, Reel retention and rewatch, profile visit then follow. Likes are weak. Hides, unfollows and reports are heavy penalties.
- Reel reach is decided in the first 1-3 seconds. Carousels get a second showing on slide 2.
- Suppressors: engagement bait ("comment YES", "tag 3 friends"), another app's watermark on reposted video, 20-30 hashtag walls, aspect ratios outside 4:5 to 1.91:1.
- Limits: caption 2,200 chars with about 125 visible before "more"; carousel 2-10 images via API, no mixed media; Reels 5-90s for the Reels tab.

## Before drafting

Get the real moment, number, or detail. Never invent one. Ask once; if the creator has nothing, drop the claim or use a [placeholder] and flag it. Pick the goal first: sends, saves, comments, or follows.

## Reels (the daily the personal account format)

All reel styles below stay in use. IG11 was added in Oct 2026 as an extra option, not a replacement.

- **IG9 Pattern-Interrupt Reel (sends):** frame-one on-screen text that breaks the expected visual or opens a loop, and a first spoken line that drops straight into the point. No "hey guys", no logo, no slow zoom.
- **IG10 How-I Reel (saves):** frame one states a specific result with a number ("how I went from [start weight] to [X] in [timeframe]"), then steps labelled on screen, then a one-line recap.
- **IG11 X-Post-Card Reel (sends, follows):** a 9:16 reel on a black background. Top: The creator's the professional account X post rendered as a post card (profile photo, name, verified tick, handle, X.com mark, post text). Bottom: the source clip (interview, podcast moment, news clip) playing under it. Fits best when an idea already has an X post, especially commentary on someone else's clip. Rules: the X post text is the hook, so it must read in 3 seconds (under ~280 chars, 2-3 short paragraphs); the clip must pay off the post inside 90s, trimmed to the moment the post references; credit the original source in the caption; no other app's watermark on the clip. Output: the X post text for the card, clip in/out note for the designer, caption (can open with the post's last line and add one extra line of the creator's take), hashtags.
- Talking-head structure: hook line (0-2s) → the tension or the claim → one proof (lived detail, number, or episode moment) → one usable takeaway → a loop-back last line that makes the rewatch natural. (Numbered series use their own structure above.)
- On-screen text on every hook so it works muted. Pay off the hook inside the Reel or the swipe-away punishes it.
- Output: on-screen hook text, spoken script with rough timestamps, B-roll or cutaway notes, caption, hashtag set.

When the creator writes an X post about a clip, offer the IG11 reel version as one option alongside the other reel styles.

## Captions (single image or Reel)

- **IG1 Number-First Result (saves):** "{Specific result} in {timeframe}." then what made it work.
- **IG2 Contrarian Truth (sends):** "{Common advice} is wrong. {What works, flat}."
- **IG3 Relatable Cold-Open (comments):** one specific shared moment, no setup.
- **IG4 Mini-Story Confession (comments, follows):** "{Time marker}: {what happened, ugly part included}." plus one line of meaning.

Rules: the first 125 characters stand alone. One idea per caption. Short lines with space between beats. One CTA that is specific ("send this to the friend who skips leg day for 'recovery'", "save this for your next cut") or a real question, never "what do you think?". 0-3 emoji placed with intent; none on a serious take.

## Carousels (the designer builds the slides)

- **IG5 Listicle (saves):** slide 1 "{N} {things} that {payoff}", one item per slide.
- **IG6 Before/After (saves, follows):** slide 1 the after, slide 2 the before, then the steps. Natural fit for the transformation.
- **IG7 Myth-Buster (sends):** "Myth: {belief}. Truth: {what works}." per slide.
- **IG8 Steal-This Framework (saves):** a named framework the creator actually uses, one part per slide.

Spine: slide 1 is a promise with an open loop, never a bare title. Strongest point on slide 2 or 3. One point per slide, readable in 2 seconds on a 4:5 frame. Last slide is a one-slide recap worth saving plus one ask. 6-10 slides, never padded. Output a slide-by-slide brief for the designer (on-image text, visual note) plus the caption.

## Hashtags

3-5 per post, sized: 2-3 niche (under ~50k posts), 1-2 mid (50k-500k), 0-1 broad. Every tag must match the content. Rotate per topic, never one pasted block. Drop engagement-farm tags. Tell the creator to verify counts in Instagram search. End of caption or first comment.

## Threads

Threads is conversation-first and warmer than X: replies are the strongest signal, then reposts and quotes. A transplanted combative X voice gets marked "not interested" faster, so keep the edge but drop the dunk.

- 500 characters per post, exactly one hashtag (topic tag) allowed, links cost reach in the opening post.
- Single-post shapes: warm contrarian; data-point hook; build-in-public confession; relatable cold-open; mini-list; **first-person status flex** (top shape in a 2026 pull: a real, flat milestone, then the counterintuitive instruction it earns); copy-swap micro-lesson (one concrete before/after line).
- Threads (4-7 posts): listicle promise, story thread, curiosity gap, how-I teardown. Post 1 promises and opens a loop; strongest item early; last post the most quotable line plus one ask.
- Repurposing an X post: soften the dunk, keep the claim and the number, add a warmer close question.

## Profile audit

Check: name field with a searchable keyword, bio that says what a follower gets plus one proof, one goal-matched link, highlights that work as a menu (transformation, podcast, the newsletter, and one highlight per running series), the top 3 pinned posts as proof of what the account delivers, profile photo readable small. Audit the personal account and the brand account separately; they serve different jobs.

## Scrub before showing anything

- No em dashes. No "Let's talk about", "Here's the thing", "POV:" on non-POVs, "real talk", "not gonna lie", "The result?", staccato stacks, one-word lines for drama.
- None of: leverage, game-changer, level up, dive in, unlock, journey, elevate, empower, "let that sink in".
- No guru framing, no hustle-bro clichés, no fake urgency. Specific over motivational.
- Nothing from the profile's hard exclusions, no prices and no product-ladder pitches.
- Every post needs media; remind the creator what to attach.

## Output

Account, series (default lane, a numbered series, or none), format, goal, formula, the content (with character count for captions and Threads posts), the hashtag set, the visual brief for the designer, and any [placeholders].
