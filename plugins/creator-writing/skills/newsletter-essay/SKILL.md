# Newsletter essay

Load `creator-profile` first. "The newsletter" below is the newsletter named there.

The newsletter is the depth layer: the place for the thinking that does not fit a post. It should read like the creator talking to a friend late at night, not like a newsletter.

## Capture the real voice first

Before drafting, read the voice sample in `PROFILE.md`. Copy what makes it theirs, not just the topic:

- **Start from a tiny real moment**, not a thesis: a coffee, a book on the shelf, a gym session, a conversation. The big idea arrives after the scene.
- **Asides in brackets** that roast the writer or add a wink. One to three per piece. Keep them.
- **Spoken rhythm.** Long run-on sentences joined the way they talk, then a short punch. Do not chop everything into tidy short sentences.
- **Casual markers** the creator really uses (ngl, btw, ofc, "wait this makes sense"). Naturally, never stacked.
- **Concrete props** over abstractions: the actual pen, the actual page.
- **Live plugs as asides** mid-flow ("btw the reel on this goes up tomorrow").

Fix real typos. If the creator writes in lowercase, keep it for the intro and asides and ask once whether to keep it for the body.

Never: corporate tone, guru framing, "In today's fast-paced world", polish that sands off the asides, invented anecdotes. If a detail of their day is needed and not given, ask or leave `[placeholder]`.

## Formats (pick one per issue)

1. **Night notes / quote POV:** one line from a book, film or conversation, then the creator's take in bites. 500 to 1,000 words.
2. **Podcast companion essay:** built on an episode of the flagship podcast. Credit the guest; never invent their words.
3. **Technical breakdown:** a real pipeline, architecture or deployment. Still in their voice, with more substance. No client names unless the profile says they are public.
4. **Operating standard:** discipline, deep work, physical training. Concrete steps told through what the creator actually does.
5. **Monthly brief:** what shipped, what they learned, one recommended read or listen.

## Default flow

1. **Scene:** the small real moment, with an aside.
2. **The thing that stuck:** quote, idea or problem, set apart on its own line with author and source.
3. **"Here are my bites":** 3 to 6 numbered points, each with one concrete example from their life.
4. **Turn it back to the reader:** one question or one thing to try.
5. **Live plugs** as a casual sign-off line, then the standard newsletter footer from `PROFILE.md`, word for word.

Always give 3 title options (a casual lowercase version and a sentence-case version) and 2 subtitle options.

## Rules

- Quotes from others short, attributed, with source. Never reproduce long passages.
- Never invent stats, quotes or life details.
- Respect the profile's em-dash and emoji rules.
- If a content calendar is set up, log the drafted issue there (see `content-calendar`).
