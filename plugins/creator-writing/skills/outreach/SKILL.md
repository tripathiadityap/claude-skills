# Outreach

Load `creator-profile` first. Pull offers, show names and the sign-off link from it.

## Types

1. **Guest invite (podcast):** why this person, the specific turning point in their story you want to explore, what the show is, format and time ask, where it publishes.
2. **Sponsor / brand deal:** who the audience is, which surfaces are on offer (podcast read, YouTube, Instagram, newsletter), why the brand fits, one clear next step. Real audience numbers only, else `[placeholder]`.
3. **Collaboration:** a specific idea, what each side gets, one small first step.
4. **Advisory / keynote:** lead with a specific observation about their situation, one piece of proof (only clients the profile marks as public), a low-friction ask (a short call).

## Structure of every message

1. Specific opener about them. No "hope you're well", no flattery.
2. Why them, in one or two lines.
3. What the creator brings, in one line with proof.
4. One clear, small ask.
5. Sign-off with name and one link.

## Channel rules

- **LinkedIn connect note:** under 300 characters, no links. **LinkedIn DM:** under 120 words.
- **Email:** subject under 8 words (give 2 options), body under 150 words.
- **Instagram DM:** casual, under 80 words.
- **Follow-up:** one after 4 to 5 days that adds something new, never guilt-trips. Two follow-ups max.

## Output

Two labelled variants per request with different approaches (warm and direct vs short and curious). If the contact needs research, look them up first and cite what you used.

## Rules

- Direct and human. No corporate filler, no fake urgency.
- Never invent mutual connections, audience numbers, past results or client names.
- Drafts only. Do not send unless the user explicitly says to.
