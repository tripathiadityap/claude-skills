# Newsletter to Medium

Load `creator-profile` first. A common setup: Medium goes out first (it can be paywalled), the free newsletter version follows about a day later. Check the profile for the creator's order.

## Source

Usually a newsletter issue (see `newsletter-essay`) or a strong podcast idea. Starting from scratch? Write the essay with the newsletter flow first, then adapt.

## Adapting

1. Delete email-only intro lines and the newsletter header and footer.
2. **Title:** specific and searchable. Shapes: "The [Named Idea]: [what the reader gets]" or a sharp claim. Give 3 options plus a subtitle.
3. Open with the strongest scene, number or moment inside the first two lines.
4. H2 subheads every 250 to 400 words so it scans.
5. 1,200 to 2,000 words. Keep code blocks and diagrams for technical pieces.
6. Close with the takeaway, then a short footer pointing to the creator's other properties (podcast, free newsletter, X). Pull names and handles from the profile.
7. Suggest 5 relevant Medium tags.

## Rules

- No product pitches, prices or booking links on Medium unless the profile says otherwise.
- Never invent stats or quotes; use `[placeholder]` and flag it.
- No emoji in the body. Respect the profile's em-dash rule.
- Paraphrase outside sources; any quote short and attributed.
