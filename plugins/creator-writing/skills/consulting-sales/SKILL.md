# Consulting sales

Load `creator-profile` first. Offers, booking link and which clients may be named all come from its "What you sell" section. For post writing also follow `linkedin-x-posts`, except where this skill overrides it.

## Core mechanic: every offer has a next step

One-off sessions do not turn into repeat clients on their own. Every listing, session close and post carries a specific next step: a call fee credited against a bigger engagement if booked within a week, a follow-up call two weeks later, a retainer, the next workshop. If the profile's offers have no ladder, propose one and mark prices as tests, never as market facts.

## Outreach sequence (LinkedIn)

- **Day 1:** connect with one genuine observation about their company. No pitch.
- **Day 3:** value DM with one specific insight. No ask.
- **Day 5:** a free audit or short call, only to people the sequence has warmed.
- Pace: about 10 connects a day.

Templates (fill the brackets, never invent the observation):

```
Connect: Hey [Name], [one line on who the creator is]. Saw [Company] is doing [X]. Have a thought on the [angle], would love to connect.

Value DM: Hey [Name], I built [relevant thing] for a similar company in [sector]. One observation about [their situation] that might be useful. Not pitching, thought it was worth sharing.

Audit offer: I do a free [length] audit for companies I want to work with. I map your current setup, find the gaps and hand you a prioritised roadmap. Zero obligation. Want to set one up?
```

## Past-client reactivation

Open with a specific observation, never "hope you are well":

```
Hey [Name], was looking at [specific thing at their company] and thought of [what we built together]. One observation on where the next win probably sits: [one line]. No pitch. Happy to send the longer version if useful.
```

After every session: recap within 24 hours, review ask in the same message, book the next session on the spot with a reason, check in on day 30.

## Booking-page listing

Title with duration, then: who it is for, what happens in the session, what they leave with, the built-in next step. Plain language, no hype, no emoji.

## Channel plays

- **LinkedIn** is the main money channel: one hard-truth or field-lesson post a week ending in the audit offer. Posts warm people up, DMs close.
- **X:** expect replies and DMs, not inbound. Pin a post saying what the creator sells, reply to the target buyers, weekly thread with the booking link in the last post.
- **YouTube:** case-study videos ("How I would audit [type of company]"), end every video with the offer, pin a comment with the booking link.
- **Podcast:** invite target buyers as guests, credit them, treat each as a warm relationship.

## Overrides of linkedin-x-posts

The booking link may go in the first comment of a sales post. Prices stay out of public posts; they live on the listing and in DMs when asked.

## Hard rules

- No engagement bait, fake urgency or guru language.
- Never invent client results, testimonials, numbers or follower counts.
- LinkedIn links in the first comment, X links in the last post.
- Say the pillar and platform before each draft.

## Output

Copy-paste ready text. Outreach: Day 1, 3 and 5 together. Listing: the full text. Posts: include the first-comment text.
