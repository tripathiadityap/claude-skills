# Daily content run

The afternoon companion to a morning brief. Morning = what changed overnight. This run = finish the day and ship tomorrow's content. It chains the other skills and delivers one digest the creator can act on in the evening. Load `creator-profile` first (timezone, accounts, IDs, exclusions).

## Hard rules

- Never send, post, publish or book anything. Drafts only (email drafts, Notion drafts, text in the digest).
- Day-job items are generic labels only ("client sync"). Never surface employer or client names or data.
- Skip anything the morning brief already covered unless it is still open.
- If a connector is missing or fails, note it in one line and continue.

## Steps

1. **Time check:** current date and time in the profile's timezone.
2. **Next up (calendar):** now until tomorrow noon. Flag podcast recordings, client calls, workshops.
3. **Needs you (email since the morning):** only real people or things that need the creator. Ignore security notices, newsletters, promotions, receipts, bulk mail. Draft up to 2 replies.
4. **Day-job admin (only if listed in the profile):** recurring items such as timesheets or attendance, on their due dates. Otherwise skip.
5. **Pipeline (`content-calendar`):** what is due today and tomorrow, whether today's daily post is ready, what waits on the designer or on the creator's review.
6. **Podcast (episode library, `podcast-guest-prep`, `podcast-episode-package`):**
   - Episodes in Draft / In production / Ready to publish with the next action. Ready to publish is top priority.
   - Live episodes missing a platform: flag the gap.
   - Live episodes with fewer than 2 clips or Shorts: pick the most under-used one and give 1 clip idea (title plus the moment to cut).
   - Recording within 48 hours: a 5-line prep (guest, angle, 3 questions).
   - Recorded but unpackaged: package it.
   - Any new derivative goes into the library as Draft with its source episode set, never Live.
7. **Read (`research-cards`):** process the 1 to 2 oldest unprocessed notes with links into Research Cards. Suggest one study for tonight.
8. **Content pack (one idea, every platform):** pick the strongest idea of the day (rotate through older podcast episodes so they keep earning, a Research Card, today's notes, or a generic work lesson). Return one version per platform: LinkedIn (`linkedin-x-posts`), X (`x-posts`), Instagram and Threads (`instagram-threads`, routed by account), YouTube (`youtube-packaging`) only if it carries a video, newsletter or Medium (`newsletter-essay`) on the creator's essay day. When the idea comes from an episode, every piece names and links it. Add each piece as a draft card via `content-calendar`.
9. **Money (`consulting-sales`):** remind the daily connects, list Day-3 and Day-5 follow-ups due, draft one outreach or reactivation message. Past podcast guests are warm contacts.
10. **Second Brain (`second-brain-daily`):** log one line: what the run processed and which idea became the content pack.

## Digest format

```
AFTERNOON RUN · <date>
NEXT UP: calendar till tomorrow noon
NEEDS YOU: replies (drafts ready)
DAY JOB ADMIN: only when due
PIPELINE: due / daily post status / designer handoffs
PODCAST: in production, distribution gaps, 1 clip idea, prep
READ: research cards filed + tonight's read
CONTENT PACK: idea: <one line> (source: <episode or card>)
  LinkedIn / X / IG / Threads / YouTube / Essay
MONEY: connects, follow-ups, 1 draft
TOP 3 FOR THE REST OF TODAY
```

Digest under about 350 words; the full content pack follows below it. No recap of steps.

## On a schedule

No one is watching: take the most reasonable reading, finish, and deliver the digest as the final message.
