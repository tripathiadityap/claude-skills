# Research cards

Turns any study, report or case study into a reusable Research Card, files it, and routes angles to the creator's platforms. Load `creator-profile` before writing any content angle.

## 1. Inputs

PDF, link, pasted text, an existing notes page, or a topic ("quick commerce unit economics"). For a topic, search and pick 2 to 4 credible sources. Prefer primary data (regulator data, filings, annual reports, offer documents) over aggregators and press.

## 2. The Research Card (every time)

- **Source:** title, publisher, date, link
- **One-line thesis:** what the study actually claims
- **Key numbers:** 3 to 6 figures, each with year and source; flag estimates vs reported
- **Business rules:** 3 to 5 reusable if-then or rule-of-thumb statements ("If CAC payback is over 18 months, growth is funded by the next round, not the customer")
- **Framework:** any model or mechanism worth naming
- **Local context:** what changes in the creator's market (payments rails, city tiers, price sensitivity, regulation, distribution)
- **Contrarian take:** where the study is weak (sample, sponsor, survivorship) or where the creator could disagree
- **So what:** one line each for content, for client or consulting work, and for the creator's own craft

Copyright: paraphrase. At most one short quote (under 15 words) per source. Never paste sections of the report.

## 3. Route angles (full drafts only when asked)

- LinkedIn (`linkedin-x-posts`), X (`x-posts`), Instagram and Threads (`instagram-threads`), YouTube (`youtube-packaging`) if it can carry a 10+ minute video, newsletter or Medium (`newsletter-essay`, `medium-adapt`), podcast (`podcast-guest-prep`: an episode theme, 3 questions or a guest type), sales (`consulting-sales`: a talking point).

## 4. File to Notion

Create a page in the notes data source from the profile: short descriptive title, a status such as "Summarised", the source link, a one-line summary (thesis plus top rule) and the full card as the body. If the user points at an existing notes page, update it and move its status forward instead of creating a duplicate.

## 5. Batch mode

"Process my notes": query notes with an early status (for example "Note Taken") that have a link, oldest first, and process up to 3 per run. Skip anything personal (relationships, journaling).

## 6. Never

- Never put an employer's or client's name or data into a card or content.
- Never invent numbers. A figure you can't verify is marked "unverified".
